## Section 1: The Content Creation System

### The 5-Step AI Content Creation Process

Every platform, every content type, every format follows the same five-step process:

1. **Brief** — Define the goal, audience, and key message before creating anything
2. **Generate** — Use AI to produce a first draft or multiple options
3. **Edit** — Refine the AI output for your brand voice and accuracy
4. **Polish** — Format for the specific platform and add any visuals
5. **Optimize** — Add the right tags, timing, and engagement hooks

The briefing step is the one most people skip. Without a clear brief, step 2 produces generic content. With a clear brief, step 2 produces something 80% of the way to done.

### The Content Brief Template

Before ANY piece of content, complete this brief:

```
Platform: [LinkedIn / Instagram / TikTok / YouTube / etc.]
Content type: [Post / Reel / Article / Thread / etc.]
Content pillar: [Which of your strategic pillars]
Target audience segment: [Which audience segment this is for]
Core message: [One sentence — what you want them to know/feel/do]
Hook strategy: [What will stop the scroll — stat/question/story/claim]
Call to action: [What you want them to do after engaging]
Brand voice note: [Any specific tone guidance for this piece]
Length/format: [Word count, aspect ratio, duration]
```

## Section 2: LinkedIn Content Creation

### The LinkedIn Algorithm today

Understanding what LinkedIn rewards today:

- **Native content** performs better than links (LinkedIn suppresses posts with external links)
- **Personal stories** from individuals outperform company page content by 5-7x
- **Comments in the first 60 minutes** dramatically amplify reach (engage with early commenters)
- **Carousels** (PDF slides) continue to generate the highest dwell time and saves
- **Video** is growing fastest — LinkedIn is actively promoting video content
- **Polls** drive high engagement but low saves — good for reach, not long-term value

### LinkedIn Posts That Consistently Work

**The Lesson Learned Format:**
```prompt
Write a LinkedIn post in my voice about a professional lesson I learned:

The situation: [describe what happened — can be success or failure]
The lesson: [what I learned that I didn't know before]
Who this helps: [my specific audience on LinkedIn — their role/challenges]

Format:
- Open with the most surprising or counterintuitive element of the lesson 
  (not "I learned an important lesson today")
- Tell the story briefly (3-4 short paragraphs)
- State the lesson explicitly
- End with a question that invites my audience to share their experience

Voice: [your voice description]
Length: 250-350 words
Use line breaks generously — single-sentence paragraphs.
No buzzwords.
```

**The Framework/System Post:**
```prompt
Create a LinkedIn carousel post (PDF document) that teaches a framework 
I use for [specific professional task].

The framework:
[Describe your system or framework in bullet points]

My audience: [who will read this]

Slide structure for a 7-slide carousel:
Slide 1: Title slide — the problem this framework solves
Slide 2: The old way vs. the new way (before/after)
Slides 3-6: One step of the framework per slide with brief explanation
Slide 7: Summary + call to action

For each slide, give me:
- Headline (under 8 words)
- 2-3 bullet points of content
- A suggested visual description

Make it educational and specific — not generic advice that could apply to anyone.
```

**The Contrarian Post:**
```prompt
Write a LinkedIn post arguing against a commonly held belief in [my industry].

The conventional wisdom I'm challenging: [state it clearly]
My actual view: [state it clearly — be direct, not wishy-washy]
My evidence or reasoning: [why I believe this]
The nuance: [what I'm NOT saying — this prevents misreading]

Format:
- Open with the conventional wisdom stated as a challenge ("Everyone says X. I disagree.")
- Make my case in 2-3 tight paragraphs
- Include one specific example or data point
- Acknowledge the strongest counterargument briefly
- Restate my position

Length: 200-250 words. Short. This format works because it's punchy.
Voice: Confident, not combative. I want people to think, not fight.
```

> 📖 **Real Example:** A supply chain consultant used the contrarian LinkedIn format to argue against the popular "just-in-time inventory reduction" position that dominated her industry. Her post, written with AI assistance in 20 minutes, accumulated 847 comments and 4,200 likes over 72 hours — her best-performing piece of content ever. The post led to 3 inbound client inquiries the same week.

## Section 3: Instagram Content Creation

### Instagram today: What Actually Works

Instagram's algorithm today prioritizes:
- **Reels** (the highest organic reach format — Instagram is incentivized to compete with TikTok)
- **Saves and shares** (indicates content so valuable they want to keep it or show others)
- **Comments** (especially comments that spark conversations in the replies)
- **Story engagement** (polls, questions, slider reactions all signal active following)
- **Profile consistency** (a coherent aesthetic increases follow conversions)

### Instagram Reels Script

```prompt
Write a 30-second Instagram Reels script for [brand/creator].

Topic: [your topic]
Hook goal: Stop the scroll in the first 3 seconds
Audience: [describe]
Tone: [educational / entertaining / inspiring / behind-the-scenes]

Script format:
[0:00-0:03] HOOK: 
Opening visual: [what's on screen]
Opening words: [first thing spoken or shown in text]

[0:04-0:08] CONTEXT:
What this video is about and why it matters to them

[0:09-0:24] CORE CONTENT:
[3-4 key points, each 3-4 seconds]
- Point 1: [visual + words]
- Point 2: [visual + words]
- Point 3: [visual + words]

[0:25-0:30] CTA:
Clear call to action (follow / comment / share / save / link in bio)

Music mood: [describe the vibe — upbeat, calm, dramatic, etc.]
Text overlay style: [minimal / caption every word / just key phrases]
```

### Instagram Caption Templates

```prompt
Write an Instagram caption for a [type of post].

Visual: [describe what the image or video shows]
Core message: [what this post is about]
Audience: [who follows this account]
Brand voice: [description]

Caption structure:
- Hook line (first line visible without "more" — must make them tap to read more)
- 2-3 sentences expanding the story or insight
- A question that makes commenting feel natural and easy
- 3-5 hashtags (specific, not generic — avoid #marketing #business)

Length: Under 150 words for the caption body.
```

## Section 4: TikTok Content Creation

### TikTok today: The Algorithm Is Entertainment-First

TikTok's algorithm is the most powerful organic reach engine in social media. A new account with zero followers can get 100,000 views on their first video if the content is right. This is unprecedented — and it rewards a specific type of content.

**What TikTok rewards:**
- Watch time to completion (if people watch 100% of your video, it gets pushed)
- Rewatches (if people watch it twice, TikTok shows it to more people)
- Comments (controversy, questions, and "wait, let me get my friends" type content)
- Shares to DMs (the ultimate signal — people sharing privately to someone specific)

**The 3-second hook is everything.** If someone scrolls past in the first 3 seconds, TikTok deprioritizes your content. Your opening is the single most important element.

### TikTok Script Formula

```prompt
Write a TikTok script for a [15/30/60]-second video about [topic].

Account type: [educational / entertainment / product / personal brand]
Target audience: [describe — age, interests, what they're looking for on TikTok]
Tone: [casual / educational / entertaining / inspiring / satisfying / controversial]

Hook options — give me 3 different opening 3-second hooks to test:
Hook A: [curiosity gap — "I learned something that changed everything about..."]
Hook B: [direct value — "3 things [audience] wish they knew about..."]
Hook C: [controversy or disagreement — "Stop doing [thing everyone does]..."]

Then write the full script for the BEST hook:

[0:00-0:03] HOOK (these words spoken aloud):
[0:04-0:08] SETUP (what this is about and why they should keep watching):
[0:09-0:XX] CONTENT (the actual teaching/entertainment/story):
[Final 3 seconds] CTA (one simple action):

Delivery note: Write this for how someone SPEAKS, not how they write. 
Short sentences. Natural rhythm. Occasional emphasis on key words.
```

### TikTok Content Series Ideas

TikTok rewards consistent series — viewers return for the next episode. Use AI to plan series:

```prompt
I want to create a TikTok content series that keeps viewers coming back.

My niche: [your topic area]
My audience: [describe]
My posting schedule: [how often]

Design a 12-episode content series with:
1. A series name and concept
2. Episode titles for all 12 (each should make someone want to see the next one)
3. A recurring format template (what stays the same every episode)
4. The series hook — what will make people follow and come back?
5. How I transition from each episode to the next (keep them watching the series)
```

## Section 5: AI-Generated Visuals for Social Media

### Midjourney V7 for Social Media Graphics

Midjourney V7 today produces photorealistic and artistic images that can replace stock photography for most social media needs.

**Effective social media image prompts:**

```prompt
[Midjourney prompt structure]

/imagine [subject description], [setting and context], [lighting and mood], 
[style/aesthetic], [technical parameters]

Example:
/imagine A professional woman in her 40s reviewing data on a tablet in a 
modern open office, natural window light from the left, confident expression, 
realistic photography style, editorial quality --ar 4:5 --v 7 --style raw
```

**Key parameters for social media:**
- `--ar 4:5` — Instagram feed aspect ratio
- `--ar 9:16` — Reels/Stories/TikTok aspect ratio
- `--ar 16:9` — YouTube thumbnail/LinkedIn header
- `--ar 1:1` — Square format
- `--style raw` — More photorealistic, less stylized
- `--no text` — Remove any text Midjourney might add

**Prompt formula for on-brand product imagery:**
```prompt
/imagine [your product] arranged on [surface], [setting that matches your brand aesthetics], 
[lighting style — soft/dramatic/natural], professional product photography, 
clean minimal composition, high resolution --ar 4:5 --v 7
```

### DALL-E 3 for Text-in-Image Graphics

DALL-E 3 (in ChatGPT Plus) is better than Midjourney at rendering accurate text within images — crucial for quote graphics, announcement posts, and educational visual content.

```prompt
[In ChatGPT with DALL-E]

Create a social media graphic with these specifications:
Text to display: "[your quote or headline]"
Visual context: [background and any imagery]
Color scheme: [your brand colors or style]
Font style: [bold/elegant/hand-written/minimal]
Format: Square (1:1)
Style: [clean and minimal / vibrant and eye-catching / warm and personal]
```

> ⚠️ **Common Mistake:** Using AI-generated images without reviewing them carefully for errors. Midjourney and DALL-E occasionally produce images with distorted hands, misrendered text, or subtle anatomical errors in human subjects. Always review AI images at full resolution before publishing.

## Section 6: The Content Repurposing Engine

### One Idea, Every Platform

The most efficient content creation systems start with one well-developed idea and systematically extract every format from it.

**The Core Repurposing Workflow:**

```prompt
I have a core content idea I want to repurpose across all my platforms.

Original format: [LinkedIn article / YouTube video / podcast episode / blog post]
Core content: [paste the content or describe it in detail]
My platforms: [list your active platforms]
Brand voice: [description]

Repurpose this into:

1. LINKEDIN POST (250 words): Extract the most counterintuitive insight; 
   make it standalone and punchy

2. INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL (7 slides): Turn the main framework or steps into 
   a swipeable educational carousel

3. INSTAGRAM REEL SCRIPT (30 seconds): The most visual, energetic element 
   adapted for Reels

4. TIKTOK SCRIPT (60 seconds): The most entertaining or surprising angle 
   adapted for TikTok's casual style

5. TWITTER/X THREAD (8 tweets): Distill the key insights into a scannable thread

6. EMAIL NEWSLETTER SECTION (300 words): More personal, conversational adaptation 
   with a direct reader application

For each format, adapt the content appropriately for the platform context — 
don't just cut it shorter. Each should feel native to that platform.
```

> 🎯 **Try This Now:** Take one piece of content you've already created (an email, a blog post, a presentation slide, a video) and run it through the repurposing prompt above. Count how many content pieces you get from one original. For most people, this exercise reveals 6-8 additional pieces from content they already have.

## Section 7: Maintaining Brand Voice at Scale

### The Brand Voice Document

When you produce content at AI-assisted scale, maintaining consistent voice requires documentation. Build a brand voice guide that travels with every content brief:

```prompt
I want to create a brand voice guide for use in AI content prompts.

Our brand personality in 3 adjectives: [describe]
The 3 adjectives we actively avoid: [what we don't want to sound like]

Examples of content that nails our voice:
[paste 2-3 examples]

Examples of content that doesn't sound like us:
[paste or describe what feels off]

Create a brand voice guide with:
1. Voice in 5 sentences (complete description of our voice and why it fits our brand)
2. Vocabulary guide: 10 words we use, 10 words we avoid
3. Sentence structure: length, complexity, rhythm preferences
4. Personality traits expressed through content
5. Platform-specific voice adjustments (how we adapt tone per platform 
   while maintaining brand consistency)

Format this so it can be pasted into a prompt as a "voice instruction" 
for any content generation task.
```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Brief before you create** — the content brief prevents generic output; without it, even the best AI model produces mediocre content.

2. **Platform algorithms reward different things** — LinkedIn rewards personal stories, Instagram rewards saves and shares, TikTok rewards watch completion. Optimize for what each platform actually rewards.

3. **Hooks are the highest-leverage creative element** — the first 3 seconds on TikTok, the first line on LinkedIn, the first image in an Instagram scroll — these determine whether anyone sees the rest.

4. **Visuals are no longer a bottleneck** — Midjourney V7 and DALL-E 3 eliminate stock photography dependency; a well-prompted AI image is often better than stock.

5. **The repurposing engine multiplies every piece** — one well-developed idea should produce content for all your platforms; this is the core efficiency gain of AI-assisted content creation.

6. **Brand voice documentation is essential at scale** — without a documented voice guide, AI-assisted content at volume becomes inconsistent and generic.

7. **Review before publishing** — AI content and AI images require human review for accuracy, appropriateness, and voice alignment; publish blind at your own risk.
